Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

Who we are

Boltbeard Games is an independent game studio. Contact: boltbeardgames@gmail.com.

Our games

Our games (including Iron Tide) store your progress and settings only on your own device. They do not collect, send or sell personal data.

Our publishing tool

We use a private tool, "Boltbeard Publisher", to upload gameplay videos of our own games to the studio's own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts and to read the view and engagement statistics of those videos. The tool is used only by the studio, only with the studio's own accounts. It does not access anyone else's account or data.

The access tokens the tool receives from YouTube (Google), Instagram (Meta) and TikTok are stored only on the studio's own computer, are used only for uploading our videos and reading their statistics, and are never shared, sold or transferred to anyone. You can revoke the tool's access at any time in your Google, Meta or TikTok account security settings.

The tool's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Videos uploaded to YouTube are subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Changes
